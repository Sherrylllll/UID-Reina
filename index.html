About Us

Reina is a Melbourne-based archival designer boutique founded in 2019 by Romy Theodore — a curator by training, a fashion obsessive by upbringing.

We specialise in designer clothing and accessories from the early 1990s through to the mid-2000s; a period when fashion was risky, colourful, and a little less serious.

Every piece is personally sourced and curated by Romy, spanning Prada, Miu Miu, Dior, Céline, Bottega Veneta, and more, alongside lesser-known names that deserve just as much attention.

You'll find us on Level 3 of Curtin House, 252 Swanston Street, Melbourne.
